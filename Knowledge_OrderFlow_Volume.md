# Advanced Order Flow & Volume Profile Knowledge Base

## 1. Volume Profile Dynamics
*   **Point of Control (POC):** The price level where the highest volume was traded for a given period. Acts as a strong magnet and fair value area.
*   **Value Area (VA):** The price range where 70% of the trading volume occurred. 
*   **Value Area High (VAH) & Value Area Low (VAL):** The upper and lower bounds of the Value Area.
*   **Volume Profile Strategy (80% Rule):** If price opens outside the Value Area, enters it, and stays inside for two consecutive 30-minute bars, there is an 80% chance it will traverse the entire Value Area to the other side (e.g., from VAH down to VAL).

## 2. Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)
*   **Delta:** The net difference between aggressive market buying volume and aggressive market selling volume at a specific price or time.
*   **CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta):** A running total of Delta.
*   **CVD Divergence (Bullish):** Price is making lower lows, but CVD is making higher lows. Indicates aggressive sellers are being absorbed by passive institutional limit buy orders. A reversal up is imminent.
*   **CVD Divergence (Bearish):** Price is making higher highs, but CVD is making lower highs. Indicates aggressive buyers are being absorbed by passive institutional limit sell orders (iceberg orders). A reversal down is imminent.

## 3. Order Book Analysis
*   **Absorption:** High volume traded at a specific price level, but price fails to move. E.g., huge market sell orders hit the tape, but the price doesn't drop. This means a large limit buyer is absorbing all the selling pressure.
*   **Exhaustion:** When price approaches a key level, but volume dries up completely. Indicates a lack of interest in pushing price further.
*   **Spoofing (Fake Liquidity):** Large limit orders placed in the order book with no intention of execution, used to trick retail traders into buying or selling, only to cancel the orders before they get filled.

## 4. Footprint Chart Concepts
*   **Imbalance:** When the buy volume at a specific price is significantly greater (usually 3x) than the sell volume on the bid, or vice versa.
*   **Stacked Imbalances:** 3 or more consecutive imbalances in a footprint candle. Acts as immediate strong support/resistance.
*   **Unfinished Auction:** When the highest or lowest price of a candle has both buy and sell volume. The market usually revisits unfinished auctions. A finished auction has zero volume on one side at the extreme high/low.
