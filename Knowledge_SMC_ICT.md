# Advanced Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & ICT Knowledge Base

## 1. Market Structure & Liquidity
*   **Break of Structure (BOS):** When price breaks a previous structural high (in an uptrend) or low (in a downtrend) and closes beyond it. Indicates trend continuation.
*   **Market Structure Shift (MSS) / Change of Character (CHOCH):** The first sign of a potential trend reversal. E.g., in a downtrend, price breaks the last lower high.
*   **Liquidity Pools:** Areas where many stop-loss orders rest. Typically above equal highs (Buy Side Liquidity - BSL) and below equal lows (Sell Side Liquidity - SSL).
*   **Liquidity Sweep (Stop Hunt):** When institutional money pushes price past a key liquidity pool just to trigger stop losses (grabbing liquidity) before sharply reversing in the intended direction.

## 2. Institutional Price Delivery
*   **Order Blocks (OB):** The last down-candle before a strong impulsive up-move (Bullish OB) or the last up-candle before a strong impulsive down-move (Bearish OB). Institutions place large orders here. Valid OBs should have an FVG immediately following them.
*   **Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Imbalance:** A three-candle pattern where the first candle's high and the third candle's low do not overlap, leaving a "gap" in price delivery. Price naturally gravitates back to FVGs to rebalance the market.
*   **Mitigation Block:** A failed Order Block that is later broken. When price returns to this broken block, it acts as support/resistance as institutions close losing positions at breakeven.
*   **Breaker Block:** Similar to a Mitigation block, but specifically occurs after a liquidity sweep (stop hunt). A Breaker is a higher probability setup than a Mitigation Block.

## 3. Premium and Discount Zones
*   Measure the current trading range from Swing High to Swing Low.
*   **Premium Zone (Above 50%):** Best area to look for short setups (Bearish OBs, FVGs). Institutions sell at a premium.
*   **Discount Zone (Below 50%):** Best area to look for long setups (Bullish OBs, FVGs). Institutions buy at a discount.
*   **Equilibrium (50%):** Fair value. Avoid trading directly in the middle of the range.

## 4. Time & Price (Killzones)
*   Institutional algorithms are time-based. High probability setups occur during specific session overlaps.
*   **London Killzone:** Often creates the High or Low of the day.
*   **New York Killzone:** High volatility, often provides a continuation or reversal of the London move.
*   **Silver Bullet:** Specific 1-hour windows (e.g., 10 AM - 11 AM NY Time) where algorithms reliably target obvious liquidity pools or FVGs.
