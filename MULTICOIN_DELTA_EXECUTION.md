# Binance-analysis → Delta multicoin execution bridge

## Scope, routing, and decision authority

The multicoin path is a separately enabled background analysis/optional execution route. It scans the configured eligible asset universe, joins each Binance spot symbol only to one unambiguous active Delta perpetual whose explicit underlying and quote/settlement metadata match the asset policy, and carries the exact provider and execution identities plus a mapping-policy ID separately. No suffix-based ticker guessing or BTC-only assumption is used in this path. Missing, ambiguous, inactive, unsupported, or policy-mismatched instruments are excluded. Per asset/timeframe, the existing analysis owner receives **500 closed candles plus the forming candle** for each supported native timeframe, with coverage/freshness checks; Parts 1–12 remain isolated per symbol. The existing India/Upstox selected-symbol route is not repurposed.

An analysis snapshot is not an order instruction. Planning requires a fresh complete result with all Parts 1–12 labels, Parts 1–10 coverage on every native timeframe, the actual deterministic Parts 11/12 outcome, a non-vetoed explicit Part 7 entry gate, and valid stop/target geometry. The code does not synthesize a setup, use price movement alone, accept LLM final decisions, or let model output override deterministic risk gates. Binance candles/reference prices are analysis-only; executable quote, product/contract metadata, collateral, order/fill state, protection state, and account reconciliation come from Delta.

## Product, quote, collateral, and risk policy

The adapter reads the complete Delta product catalogue through bounded cursor pagination. It accepts only explicitly identified active perpetual products with exact IDs/symbols, underlying, quote and settlement assets, supported linear/vanilla denomination metadata, positive tick size and contract value/unit, and operational trading state. Contract notional is computed only from explicit supported contract-unit metadata. Inverse/coin-margined products, unsupported units, ambiguous metadata, quote/settlement mismatches, and instruments without an explicit asset-level risk policy fail closed.

Executable prices come from the exact Delta L2 book (top bid/ask and exchange timestamp), with identity, crossed-book, future-time and stale-time checks. Buys use ask; sells use bid. Available collateral is read from exactly one matching `asset_symbol` wallet row and its `available_balance`. **No USD/USDT/INR/coin conversion is inferred or substituted.** The compatibility `get_available_balance_usdt()` only reads an actual USDT row. A trade's risk currency must equal both its quoted and settlement currency; the coordinator rejects portfolios combining currencies because it has no validated FX/risk aggregation adapter. `jarvis_risk.calculate_trade_size` plus `jarvis_lot_limits.enforce_entry_lots` perform contract-value and whole-contract sizing under configured risk/leverage limits. The order adapter sets leverage and reads it back from the documented per-product leverage endpoint before entry.

## Staged order and protection lifecycle — explicitly non-atomic

Delta's documented bracket endpoint is a **separate operation** attached after an entry has filled. The implementation therefore does not claim atomic entry-plus-protection and has no unprotected-market fallback. It:

1. Rechecks all five live opt-ins, mainnet client selection, kill switch, exact product policy, fresh Delta quote/geometry, supported contract value and confirmed leverage.
2. Sends a stable bounded client-order-ID market entry; unknown submit responses remain `SUBMISSION_UNKNOWN` and retain their journal reservation.
3. Cancels any unfilled remainder and re-reads the order before proceeding. It requires a positive, exactly matched position size and average fill price.
4. Posts the separate full-position TP/SL bracket and independently paginates/validates exactly one active reduce-only stop child and one active reduce-only take-profit child for the exact product and close side.
5. Returns a protected fill only after both children are visible. On bracket failure, incomplete verification, or missing children it attempts a deterministic reduce-only emergency market close, but keeps the outcome unknown until authoritative reconciliation proves the account state.

The documented bracket example acknowledges creation with only `{success: true}`; it does not prove atomicity or subsequent activation. Its stop uses a stop-limit order, so a market gap or limit non-fill can leave residual exposure. The monitor checks fresh Delta quotes and owned position/exit state; a close is exact-identity and reduce-only, and neither a close acknowledgement nor a missing position alone releases the reservation. Operators still need venue-aware monitoring and an independent emergency process.

## Account reconciliation and boundedness

`get_complete_account_snapshot` requires a complete product catalogue, wallet response, margin-position response, exact product-scoped position reads for the active perpetual universe, and complete bounded order pagination. Product reconciliation is capped by `JARVIS_DELTA_MAX_RECONCILE_PERPETUALS` (default 100, maximum 250); pagination has fixed page ceilings and cursor-loop detection. Open positions are valued only in their explicit settlement currency. Any unsupported open position, multiple settlement currencies, unknown/malformed owned entry or close, non-perpetual open account position, missing bracket child, or inconsistent direction/size makes the snapshot incomplete. Unowned active orders are surfaced as external orders and block coordinator readiness. Delta's `positions/margined` endpoint is also checked, while exact `/v2/positions?product_id=...` reads are used because the documentation warns the margined view can lag.

The journal persists candidate and close order identities, reservations and partial/unknown state. Startup reconciliation is mandatory; concurrent candidate submission is serialized; duplicates are idempotent; owned closes stay pending until the complete broker snapshot verifies termination. India/Upstox code paths are unchanged.

## Activation and validation boundaries

Both analysis and multicoin execution are off by default. `JARVIS_MULTICOIN_ANALYSIS=1` opts into isolated analysis only. Execution initialization additionally requires its own `JARVIS_MULTICOIN_DELTA_EXECUTION=1`, a reviewed per-asset policy, and clean startup reconciliation. Each concrete Delta entry and close independently requires `JARVIS_AUTO_TRADE=1`, `JARVIS_LIVE_EXECUTION=1`, `DELTA_USE_MAINNET=1`, `DELTA_ORDER_EXECUTION_ENABLED=1`, `JARVIS_MULTICOIN_DELTA_EXECUTION=1`, the mainnet-selected wrapper, and a clear kill switch. Those flags are authorization gates, not a recommendation or certification to trade.

Offline tests mock only the REST transport boundary and exercise actual wrapper parsers/request orchestration, plus a non-BTC ETH candidate through the adapter, protected fill, monitor, reduce-only close, and restart reconciliation. These tests do not access credentials or exchange endpoints and do not certify Delta response behavior. The repository does not establish current product coverage, account tier/margin semantics, wallet-row freshness, endpoint permissions/rate limits, actual order state transitions, bracket activation/ownership behavior, or live fill/cancel/reconciliation behavior for this account or every India contract. Official documentation is evidence for the described endpoint shapes, not proof that the current production account responds identically. No live/testnet request, account lookup, order, deployment, or readiness approval was performed; retain draft/review status and a separate operational kill-switch procedure.
