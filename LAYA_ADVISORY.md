# Experimental Laya advisory integration

The adapter is connected to Jarvis's terminal `JARVIS DECISION AUDIT` at `_print_decision_audit` in `jarvis_FIXED.py`, called after the final post-gate snapshot is built. It emits status and (when available) a suggestion as explicitly non-authoritative commentary. It never edits the final decision or participates in order submission/gates. The runtime wrapper snapshots the compact, same-symbol decision context and starts at most one daemon inference worker globally. The trading loop does not wait for model import, checkpoint download, or prediction. A busy worker is reported as `pending`; completion/unavailable/error/invalid/disabled states are reported distinctly at later audit output. Model call failures remain advisory-only.

`JARVIS_LAYA_ADVISORY=true` by default; set `false` to disable. The optional `laya` package must be installed (`python -m pip install laya`) to produce predictions; absent package is shown as unavailable. Router/predict is lazy and may load/download a checkpoint on first use, but only inside the daemon. No Laya checkpoint was downloaded and no inference/live trading/order was performed. Upstream source confirms `Router.predict(state, questions, ...)` and lazy checkpoint load on prediction (`NandhaKishorM/laya`, `laya/router.py`); do not treat general-purpose Laya as market-trained or validated. Confidence/advice are experimental.

Existing Ollama paths are NOT all replaced. In `jarvis_FIXED.py`, `_get_deepseek_validation` around line 6567 is an active Ollama-required entry veto outside pure-algorithm mode; keep its fail-closed gate untouched. Options tracker `analyze_options_with_ollama` around 3690 is disabled by pure-algo mode but active otherwise. Other local model callers (`_call_ollama_local` around 3833 and AI helper classes around 3863+) also remain. Existing pure-algorithm checks (including the deepseek synthesis guard around 6162 and options guard) remain unchanged. Parts/options side paths were not changed. This PR does not claim repository-wide Ollama replacement.

Offline tests: `python -m unittest -v test_jarvis_laya_advisor.py test_jarvis_laya_runtime.py` (no Laya package, model, or network required).
